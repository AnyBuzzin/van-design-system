# VanDesign product direction

Present capability and blockers belong in [PROJECT_STATUS.md](../PROJECT_STATUS.md); working rules belong in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). Work top to bottom; each item is done when it works in Fusion.

Deliver these outcomes through small verified increments:

1. One-command live Fusion test: a Runner job that creates a fresh document, builds a named model, runs its checks, saves a screenshot, closes without saving and prints PASS/FAIL. Every later item is tested with it.
2. Build the existing bed/cabinet composition together in Fusion; measure solids, cuts, contacts and clearance.
3. Demonstrate combined size/position changes and restoration, rechecking collisions.
4. Fit furniture to the owner's confirmed vehicle and measured envelope, including access and wheel arches.
5. Add one useful drawer/storage module.
6. Support 5, 10, 12, 15 and 18 mm plywood where appropriate, including tolerances and valid joinery.
7. Export individual panel contours; validate tooling allowances, assembly and anchoring before claiming CNC readiness.

Keep the existing core/adapter/Runner. Interlocking assembly and secure vehicle anchoring remain product goals requiring further qualification. Later layout automation and manufacturing traceability follow demonstrated needs.

This page records direction, not a duplicate milestone log or blanket release/manufacturing approval. Follow [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) for workflow.
