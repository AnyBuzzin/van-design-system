# V4 and V5 dado regression checklist

1. Build new empty Fusion design in parametric mode.
2. With W=1200, Ply=18, Dado=6, end clearance=0.3 mm: shelf length=1175.4 mm, left/right insertion=5.7 mm.
3. Change Dado to 10 mm: shelf length=1183.4 mm, insertion=9.7 mm.
4. Change W to 1000 mm: shelf length=983.4 mm, insertion=9.7 mm.
5. Restore W=1200 and change Ply=15 mm: shelf length=1189.4 mm.
6. Validate shelf position, slot height, clearances, physical grooves, no unintended cut, and zero interference.
7. Save, close and reopen: verify stable identities and regenerated parameter state.
8. Keep report and screenshot with date/Fusion version; UNKNOWN is never PASS.
